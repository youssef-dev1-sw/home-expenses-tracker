Ehsebha - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Data Storage

Ehsebha is a household expense and budget tracking app. Expenses, notes, categories, dates, and budget amounts entered by users are stored locally on their devices.

Data Collection and Sharing

The app developer does not collect or receive the expense and budget information entered into the app. This information is not sold or shared by the app developer.

Data Deletion

Users can delete their data within the app, clear the app storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling the app may permanently delete locally stored information.

Security

Data is stored locally on the user's device. Users should protect their devices using available security features.

Children's Privacy

Ehsebha is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
